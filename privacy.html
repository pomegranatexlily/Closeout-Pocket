Privacy & Data

Packet Lily is an early, browser-only prototype for making job completion packets. It does not currently offer accounts, subscriptions, cloud synchronization, a central job database, or automatic customer emails. The Email invoice control opens an addressed draft in your email app; you must review it and press Send. The app can also include an optional Cash App link supplied by the operator in an email or a message shared using the device's share sheet. The operator may explicitly save a reusable business profile, including its business address and $cashtag, in this browser. New invoices reuse that profile automatically. Amount-specific Cash App requests must be created and confirmed individually. The saved profile is not a connected Cash App account and is never a credential. The app does not connect to Cash App, move money, or verify payments; invoice balances must be updated manually after payment.

What you enter

You may choose to enter business and customer names, contact details, service addresses, job descriptions, photos, invoice references, and payment amounts. When the operator enables device saving, the app keeps drafts and invoices in this device's browser local storage, using the keys closeout-pocket-draft-v1 and closeout-pocket-saved-v1. Generating a Job Packet archives it in this browser only when device saving is enabled. Older stored records remain available until erased. These records are not sent to a Packet Lily application server by the current code. You can export a JSON backup with saved customer details and photos, then import that backup later. Protect exported backups as sensitive customer records.

Photos and exported reports

Photos are processed in your browser. When you choose to download, share, copy, or print a report, you control where it goes. A self-contained downloaded report may include customer details and photos. Take care before sending it to another party. Browser storage has limits and is not an encrypted, guaranteed backup; export anything important.

Hosting and third parties

GitHub Pages delivers the site and may process ordinary network information such as IP addresses, browser identifiers, and request logs according to GitHub's own practices. The prototype does not intentionally load external analytics, advertisements, fonts, or runtime libraries. No marketing cookies are installed by its application code.

Important limitation on shared GitHub hosting

If hosted at pomegranatexlily.github.io/Closeout-Pocket/, other projects under the same pomegranatexlily.github.io hostname share the same browser origin. Code running on that origin may technically access browser local storage across project paths. Use invented customer details for testing. Do not store real confidential customer records until Packet Lily has a separately isolated hostname and appropriate security safeguards.

Storage choices and cookies

Packet Lily uses no analytics, advertising trackers or nonessential cookies, so an “Accept all cookies” banner is unnecessary. The optional local saving preference is stored under closeout-pocket-privacy-v1. When Save business details is selected under My Business, the app stores that reusable profile under closeout-pocket-business-v1, independently of whether invoice archiving is enabled. It contains business contact and payment-profile details, not payment credentials. Use Clear saved profile on My Business to remove it, or clear this site's browser storage. This profile is not included in invoice backup exports, and erasing invoices does not erase it. The Invoice archive lets operators pause invoice saving, export backups, or erase drafts and invoices. Pausing invoice saving does not erase existing records.

Offline support

A service worker caches only public application files, never customer invoices, photographs or backups. Cash App payment requests and email sharing require their external services.

Your controls

Use the app’s Erase all local invoices and drafts control, or your browser's site-data settings, to clear local records. You can also remove saved packets using the app controls. Avoid clearing browser data before exporting information you need. Opening the same app in another browser or installed app window may not share existing drafts.

This description reflects the October 2026 prototype, not a legal compliance certification. Data practices must be reassessed before registration, cloud storage, or paid plans launch.
