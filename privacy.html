Privacy & Data

Closeout Pocket is an early, browser-only prototype for making job completion packets. It does not currently offer accounts, subscriptions, cloud synchronization, a central job database, or automatic customer emails. The Email invoice control opens an addressed draft in your email app; you must review it and press Send. The app can also include an optional Cash App link supplied by the operator in an email or a message shared using the device's share sheet. The app does not connect to Cash App, move money, or verify payments; invoice balances must be updated manually after payment.

What you enter

You may choose to enter business and customer names, contact details, service addresses, job descriptions, photos, invoice references, and payment amounts. The app is designed to keep drafts and invoices in this device's browser local storage, using the keys closeout-pocket-draft-v1 and closeout-pocket-saved-v1. Generating a Job Packet attempts to archive it in this browser. These records are not sent to a Closeout Pocket application server by the current code. You can export a JSON backup with saved customer details and photos, then import that backup later. Protect exported backups as sensitive customer records.

Photos and exported reports

Photos are processed in your browser. When you choose to download, share, copy, or print a report, you control where it goes. A self-contained downloaded report may include customer details and photos. Take care before sending it to another party. Browser storage has limits and is not an encrypted, guaranteed backup; export anything important.

Hosting and third parties

GitHub Pages delivers the site and may process ordinary network information such as IP addresses, browser identifiers, and request logs according to GitHub's own practices. The prototype does not intentionally load external analytics, advertisements, fonts, or runtime libraries. No marketing cookies are installed by its application code.

Important limitation on shared GitHub hosting

If hosted at pomegranatexlily.github.io/Closeout-Pocket/, other projects under the same pomegranatexlily.github.io hostname share the same browser origin. Code running on that origin may technically access browser local storage across project paths. Use invented customer details for testing. Do not store real confidential customer records until Closeout Pocket has a separately isolated hostname and appropriate security safeguards.

Your controls

Use your browser's site-data settings to clear local drafts and saved packets. You can also remove saved packets using the app controls. Avoid clearing browser data before exporting information you need. Opening the same app in another browser or installed app window may not share existing drafts.

This description reflects the October 2026 prototype, not a legal compliance certification. Data practices must be reassessed before registration, cloud storage, or paid plans launch.
